# Spike S3: Street View density, region distribution and Google API quotas

Date: 2026-11-13
Goal: verify the weighted country selection algorithm without repeats within a match, the calculation of region diagonals (D) from Natural Earth 110m, and the free-quota limits of Google Maps Platform.

## 1. Country weighting (`area^0.65`) and Natural Earth 110m data

The `scripts/build-regions.ts` generator processes `world-atlas/countries-110m.json`:

- Antarctica (`010`) is excluded.
- Russia is split at `60°E` into `europe` and `asia`; French Guiana is assigned to `south_america`.
- Russian names (`NAME_RU`) are filled in for 175 countries.
- The diagonal $D$ is computed from the minimal bounding bbox, accounting for crossing the 180th meridian (`world`, `asia`, `oceania`).

## 2. Threshold of 20+ countries per country and the fallback (120 km)

- For the regions `world` (175), `africa` (51), `asia` (48) and `europe` (39), the number of available countries far exceeds the maximum number of rounds in a match (15), which guarantees selection without repeats.
- For `south_america` (14) and `oceania` (7), a safe fallback is provided when `rounds > countries.length` (once the unique countries are exhausted, a repeat within the match is allowed).
- The coastal/border lookup `findCountryAtPoint(pos)` applies a tolerance of **120 km** to the distance to polygon vertices when a point lands imprecisely near the coastline.

## 3. Google Cloud Console quotas and budget protection

Under the current Google Maps Platform rules (Essentials / free tier per month with no charges):

| Metric                                      | Google free threshold / month | Ceiling in `quota.json` (`quotaCeilings`) | Recommended daily limit in Console |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------: | ----------------------------------------: | ---------------------------------- |
| Street View Static API (`staticRequests`)   |                        10,000 |                           **8,000** (80%) | 500 req/day                        |
| Street View Dynamic / JS Pano (`panoLoads`) |                         5,000 |                           **4,000** (80%) | 250 loads/day                      |
| Maps JavaScript API (`mapLoads`)            |                        10,000 |                           **8,000** (80%) | 500 loads/day                      |

When creating panoramas and loading maps:

- The worst-case calculation before a match starts checks all 3 counters, taking into account the number of players, bots and rounds (`calculateWorstCaseMatchCost`).
- The browser reports the actual `pano_loaded` and `map_loaded` events (T53) when an instance is initialized during the match.
- The `metadata` filter when choosing a location requires `status === 'OK'`, `source=outdoor` and `Google` in `copyright` (excluding user-made photospheres without a Google panorama graph).
